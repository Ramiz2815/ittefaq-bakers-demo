A Rawalpindi counter that's rarely quiet

Ittefaq Bakers & Sweets sits at Muhammadi Chowk, and most evenings there's a small line out the door — for a birthday cake ordered that morning, a box of sweets for a guest arriving in an hour, or just warm bread on the way home.

The bakery runs late, closing at 3 AM, because Rawalpindi doesn't stop wanting fresh bread and dessert just because the sun's gone down. Everything on the counter — cakes, pastries, biscuits, sweets, bread, donuts and a small fast-food menu — is made in-house, most of it that same day.

Orders

Orders placed through this site are requests sent to the shop for confirmation by phone or WhatsApp. Prices shown are indicative and may change slightly based on weight, size or customisation requested.

Custom cakes

  • Custom designs and message icing need at least 4 hours' notice where possible.
  • Reference images help, but hand-piped icing will vary slightly from any picture.
  • Confirm flavour, size and pickup time when the shop calls to confirm your order.

Payment

Cash on pickup or delivery is accepted at the counter. Any online payment options will be confirmed directly by the shop when your order is confirmed.

Delivery & pickup

Pickup is available from the Muhammadi Chowk counter during opening hours. Delivery availability and charges depend on your area and are confirmed at the time of order.

Cancellations

Standard items can be cancelled before preparation begins. Custom cakes and made-to-order items, once in progress, generally can't be cancelled or refunded — call the shop as early as possible if plans change.

Allergens

Products are prepared in a kitchen that handles nuts, dairy, eggs and gluten. Let the shop know about any allergy before ordering.
